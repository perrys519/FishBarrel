Country

AI auto-detect (Beta)

FishBarrel can scan each page you visit with Chrome's on-device AI (Gemini Nano) and pre-fill suspected misleading health claims for you. Everything runs locally — no page text leaves your computer.

Model status: Checking…

The on-device model needs Chrome 138 or later and a device with at least 22 GB free disk and 16 GB RAM (or a 4+ GB-VRAM GPU). See Chrome's built-in AI docs.

Advanced — prompt template
